# FocusLens: what the assistant knows about the app

FocusLens helps secondary school students in Egypt study with real focus and actually understand the lesson, and lets parents follow progress without standing over them.

## Starting out
- On first launch the student picks what they need help with most: focus better, catch up, prepare for exams, or build a routine.
- Home shows "Start your First Session" for new users, and "Start a New Session" afterwards, plus a weekly study overview.

## Study sessions
- A session is a focused block of study, 25 minutes by default.
- Two modes:
  - **Paper**: studying from a book, notebook or printed material, away from the screen.
  - **Digital**: studying a PDF, website or video inside FocusLens.
- **Phone lock**: while a session runs, the phone is locked into FocusLens. Notifications are silenced and the student can't leave the app until the session ends. This is on purpose, so the time is real focus time.
- The student can Pause or End the session. Ending early is allowed but is recorded.
- **Overtime**: if the timer reaches 00:00 and the student keeps going, FocusLens counts the extra time as overtime.

## Micro-challenges
- During a session FocusLens shows short required questions (micro-challenges) built from the student's own material.
- They check understanding, not memorising. The student must answer to continue.
- "Add 5 min" gives a little more time on the current section before the challenge.
- Results feed the Understanding score.

## Session history
- Every finished session appears in Session History with subject, date, duration, mode, Focus level and Understanding level.
- Filters: subject, study mode (Digital / Paper) and date range.
- Session Details shows a summary, highlights (duration, focus, pauses) and a full report.
- **Reuse session content** starts a new session with the same uploaded file and sections. The old session's results are kept.

## Progress
- Progress Analytics shows Study Sessions, Focus Quality and Understanding for the week, month or all time.
- Focus Quality combines sustained study time, pause frequency and completing required micro-challenges. It reflects study consistency, not academic ability.
- Understanding uses micro-challenge accuracy, retries and section-level performance.
- Study Sessions counts completed sessions. Paused or abandoned sessions are not included.

## Parents
- A parent can send a connection request. The student sees it in Notifications and can Accept or Decline. The student can disconnect anytime.
- A parent can propose a weekly study goal (for example 300 minutes). Once accepted, completed sessions count toward it.
- Privacy & Sharing in Profile lets the student choose what the parent sees: Share Session Summary and Share Subject Trends. The student is in control.

## Notifications
- Tabs: All, Unread, System. Includes study reminders, session summaries, tips, parent requests and weekly progress.

## Profile
- Edit Profile: name, email, grade level, interests/subjects, profile photo.
- Log out from Profile.

## The assistant itself
- Students get a daily question allowance that resets every day at midnight (Cairo time). Paid plans get more.
- The assistant only helps with school learning and with using FocusLens.

## Things the assistant must not invent
- Prices, release dates, features not listed here. If unsure, say the team will add it soon or that you don't know.
